How to play

Start with 6 cards. Draw 4 each turn and spend 3 energy. Unplayed cards go to the discard pile at turn end; discarded cards shuffle back when needed. Block expires after the enemy attacks. Enemy intent shows exact incoming damage. Enemies gain 1 attack every two turns, so you cannot defend forever. Win, choose a reward or skip it, then trim your deck before the next fight. Battles save after every action in this browser, separately from the adventure.
