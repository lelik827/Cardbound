Rules

Build a 4–10 card active deck from your card pool. Every card begins at Level 0 and needs 50 plays before each of its three upgrades can be unlocked. A ready card also requires one upgrade material found in the open-world adventure. Every third battle is a boss. Bosses have a 10% chance to drop a Soulbound card, which can never be destroyed or lost.
